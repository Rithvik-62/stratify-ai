## 🎬 Step-by-Step Presenter Script & Navigation Map

### 📍 PERSON 1: Business Problem & Platform Vision (0:00 - 5:00)
- **Screen Navigation**: Open the main presentation title slide, then switch to the **5-Stage Solution Map**.
- **Key Message**: Traditional business reporting is slow, manual, and reactive. Stratify AI unifies data into instant strategic decision intelligence.
- **Word-for-Word Hand-off Script**:
  > *"Thank you everyone. Now, to explain how our store sales are captured in real-time and filtered through an automated digital quality check before touching any business report, I'll hand over to Person 2."*

---

### 📍 PERSON 2: Live Store Capture & Smart Data Quality (5:00 - 10:00)
- **Screen Navigation**: Point to the **Digital Quality Control Filter** architecture diagram and the **Clean Repository Status**.
- **Key Message**: Every business decision requires 100% verified data. We eliminate 'Garbage In, Garbage Out' through automated deduplication and quality validation.
- **Word-for-Word Hand-off Script**:
  > *"Now that we have established a continuous flow of 100% clean, verified store data, I will hand over to Person 3 to show how we extract customer intelligence, forecast future sales, and track inventory health."*

---

### 📍 PERSON 3: Customer Intelligence & Sales Growth Forecasting (10:00 - 15:00)
- **Screen Navigation**: Display the **RFM Customer Segmentation Matrix** (Champions vs. At-Risk) and the **30-Day Sales Forecasting Chart**.
- **Key Message**: Raw sales data is converted into actionable customer cohorts and accurate revenue projections to protect profit margins and prevent stockouts.
- **Word-for-Word Hand-off Script**:
  > *"Now that we've seen how clean data transforms into customer insights and sales forecasts, let's pass to Person 4 to experience our interactive, real-time Executive Control Center."*

---

### 📍 PERSON 4: Executive Control Center & Live Store Monitoring (15:00 - 20:00)
- **Screen Navigation**: Switch screen live to the **Streamlit Web Application** (`app.py`), showing the **Live Transaction Feed** and **Color-Coded Health Scores**.
- **Key Message**: Leadership gets a single-screen command portal with 9 business hubs, real-time store transaction tickers, and 5-second health checks.
- **Word-for-Word Hand-off Script**:
  > *"While visual charts show us what is happening across our stores, I will now hand over to Person 5 to explain how our built-in AI strategy advisor tells us WHY it happened and WHAT strategic actions to take."*

---

### 📍 PERSON 5: AI Strategy Advisor & Plain English Q&A (20:00 - 25:00)
- **Screen Navigation**: Navigate to the **AI Insights Hub**, demonstrating the **Natural Language Q&A Interface** and **Action Recommendation Cards**.
- **Key Message**: Integrated DeepSeek AI acts as a 24/7 Chief Data Officer, letting non-technical managers ask questions in plain English and receive instant strategy guidance.
- **Word-for-Word Hand-off Script**:
  > *"Now that we've seen how AI turns data into strategic advice, I'll hand over to Person 6 to show how these insights are automatically compiled into executive PDF briefings and delivered straight to leadership inboxes."*

---

### 📍 PERSON 6: Automated Executive Reporting & Business ROI (25:00 - 30:00)
- **Screen Navigation**: Display the **8-Page Executive PDF Briefing**, the **RPA Email Dispatch Flow**, and the final **Business ROI Summary**.
- **Key Message**: Zero-touch automated PDF briefings delivered straight to C-suite Gmail inboxes every morning, saving 15+ hours/week per manager and driving top-line growth.
- **Closing Script**:
  > *"That concludes our 6-part presentation of Stratify AI. We would now like to open the floor to any questions from the executive team!"*

3. **If asked about Ease of Use**: Refer to **Person 4's** 9-Hub Streamlit Dashboard and **Person 5's** Plain English Q&A.
4. **If asked about Time Savings & ROI**: Refer to **Person 6's** 15+ hours/week saved metric and automated RPA email briefs.
